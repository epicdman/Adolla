About Pulsar

Pulsar is the internal tool Adolla uses to publish and schedule videos on its own YouTube channels. It runs on our own computer and is used only by Adolla. It is not available to the public.

Pulsar signs in with Google so it can act on Adolla's own accounts. How that data is handled is in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using it are in the Terms of Service.

  • YouTube: uploads finished videos to our own channels and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): reads our own calendar so publishing and work get scheduled around what's already booked.
  • Gmail: drafts business email in our own mailbox, sends it only after we approve it, and reads the replies.
  • Nothing is published or sent without a person approving it first.
